# Startup x87 precision, investigation 09

The pinned executable (SHA-256 `0c78e7da6d0bcb4603fa179449bbe6d1b6b5e1c627a22c445d14d489bd9bff86`) requests **53-bit x87 precision during normal CRT startup**, before application entry. The x87 control word at match or keeper entry remains unobserved. Addresses below are virtual addresses.

The PE32 header specifies image base `00400000` and entry RVA `00547a13`, yielding entry `00947a13`. At `00947aa6` the entry calls `00945907`; the latter loads a callback from `00ad6c14` and calls it at `00945910` when nonzero. The original PE DWORD at `00ad6c14` is `009455b2`. That initializer calls `0094a698` at `009455c1`, before the entry calls application routine `0068d370` at `00947adc`. The relevant instruction sites and call relationship were checked against the saved native Ghidra project and original PE bytes.

`0094a698` supplies value `0x10000` under mask `0x30000` through `0094f5b0` and `0094f57b`. The latter saves the 16-bit control word, merges the requested abstract precision field, and calls `0094f658` to map it to hardware bits. At `0094f6bd..0094f6cf`, value `0x10000` selects precision-control bits `0x0200`; `0094f5a8` loads the resulting word. The following `FNCLEX` clears exception flags. This supports 53-bit precision **immediately after this setter** on the normal startup path. It does not establish a complete control word of `0x027f`: other mask, rounding and control bits are preserved from the prior word.

Several later control-word changes are temporary on inspected paths. The widely called `00945640` changes rounding for conversion at `00945656`, then restores the saved word at `0094565c`. The `00950140` helper temporarily selects 64-bit precision at `0095019a`/`00950208` and restores saved words on observed exits. Other math helpers similarly restore saved state. This is evidence about those paths, not closure over all calls or threads.

There is a concrete alternative: `00952ee2` loads a complete supplied control word at `00952efc`. Its caller can pass a word modified after a floating-point exception record is handled (`00950f5e` through `00952c79`). A handler-modified precision field could change the later precision. The `00945907` startup initializer tables also contain 43 nonzero callbacks after the setter, and application execution, imports, environment restores and execution-thread identity have not been traced through match entry. The static control-site inventory in `evidence/float-control-sites.json` omits environment restoration instructions such as `FRSTOR` at `0094612e`.

Therefore, keep both 53-bit (`0x027f` fixture) and 64-bit (`0x037f` fixture) keeper comparisons as **fixture modes**. Neither fixture word is a measured match-entry word. A runtime trace should record the full x87 control word and thread identity at match/keeper entry, then follow any later setter before narrowing the simulation contract. The existing finite helper remains scoped to its tested arithmetic.

This report adds static startup provenance and two native comments; it adds no original-instruction oracle, TypeScript behavior or complete match claim. Its native source exports stay private.
